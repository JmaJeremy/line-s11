LINE S11
Barrie south to Allandale
Using existing dynamics, we can provide a speedy express, under 10 minutes, from Barrie South to Allandale
Due to our Budd RDC trains being shorter and lighter, the 7min30sec ride that GO Trains currently do, can be beaten simply by the physics and size of our trains
 
BARRIE TRANSIT ROUTE 11
Barrie-Allandale to Downtown Barrie Terminal or an alternative station
Use the two Battery Electric buses exclusively on this route
Eventually propose it for a Tram Conversion, with LINX Rail then taking over the service
Using these two buses is using rolling stock we already have, and allows for us to provide a Lakeshore bus service that can serve the Beach on the way to Downtown, and then terminate at the Terminal or other alternative location
 
SOUTH END BARRIE LOCAL BUSES
We can propose that SADC/Bus Simcoeland’s Subsidiary BSZ Regio, could operate the South End services out of Barrie South
These Autarian run buses can link the Painswick area the Station
We would let BT have a route from Barrie South to Park Place via Mapleview Drive, while BSZ-REGIO will operate all the feeder buses that circulate Painswick, and also a few old routes, like a route from Barrie South to Park Place via Dean, Big Bay Point and Huronia Road, and the old Hurst Drive routes from Downtown to Allandale and Barrie South, which we’d choose to number 38 and 39, and offer to operate a combo of routes, the 33 Innisdale and 32 Bayview, which use Allandale Rec Centre and Downtown Barrie Terminal as their hubs.
 
LOCAL BUSES ALLANDALE-DOWNTOWN
Barrie Transit would have the rights to run Downtown-Allandale on Lakeshore Drive and Bradford Street
BSZ-R could offer service on Innisfil Street and Anne Street South via the same trajectory,  and perhaps propose to use Highway 400 via Essa and Bayfield to reach Downtown.
 
The key, is we know and understand Barrie Transit is hamstrung for buses
So if SADC can use our incubator bus company as a vehicle to deliver the service people want and need, and back up our rail proposal, we’d love to do it. We see ourselves as a partner, not a competitor.
This rail project is a partnership anyhow, and we at SADC wouldn’t just want to make the County and City pay the freight. Even if we can’t contribute money, we can ask to be given some money, which we can spend on buses to provide labour and service, in lieu of cash to the project.
 
This rail project is aimed to be a gamechanger, and even if we don’t beat Eglinton, at the very least, we can launch a working service by Spring of 2026, and get an epic win through the low cost rapid transit we’ve developed, and  doing it in a way that doesn’t take 20 years or a trillion dollars, and most importantly, is reliable. We do it by using stuff we already have, with the only capital being vehicles and labour.
